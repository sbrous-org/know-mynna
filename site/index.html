Questions

Good to know

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the link and you're in. Your device is remembered, so reopening the page puts you back in the same trip.

How many friends can join?

Up to 8 people per trip. The host can approve or remove members, pause joining, or reset the invite link at any time.

Can I trust the permit and season advice?

Mynna answers from a curated guide and flags permit and season information as “verify before you travel”. Rules change often, so always confirm with the official source.

Can I keep the plan after the trip chat ends?

Yes. Download a readable plan or print it as PDF, or save a trip file and load it into a new trip later.

Does Mynna book hotels or transport?

Not today. Mynna helps your group decide where to go and in what order, then hands you a plan to book with whoever you like.
